Math and code

Inline math sits in the line: \(e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0\). Display math stands alone:

$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$$ 
def slope(f, x, h=1e-6):
    """Approximate the derivative of f at x."""
    return (f(x + h) - f(x)) / h

Checkpoints

Checkpoint 1

What is the derivative of \(x^2\)?

Hint

Expand \((x+h)^2\) inside the definition.

Solution

\(\frac{(x+h)^2 - x^2}{h} = 2x + h \to 2x\).
